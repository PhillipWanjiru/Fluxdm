# Code of conduct

FluxDM is a technical project, and technical work goes best when people can disagree about ideas without
being disrespected as people.

## What is expected

- Be courteous and assume good faith. Critique the code, the design or the argument, never the person.
- Be specific and be kind: say what is wrong, why, and what would fix it.
- Accept review the way you give it. A review comment is about the change, not about you.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone here was new once.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning language about who someone is.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete contributions that break these rules, and may restrict or remove
people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, use GitHub's private
[report a vulnerability](https://github.com/PhillipWanjiru/Fluxdm/security/advisories/new) form and say that
it concerns conduct, or contact the maintainer through the address on their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled privately.
